How we counted this

Each period is a stretch of time when an Executive was in office, from the day it was formed to the day it was suspended, collapsed or dissolved. Everything in between counts as no Executive, including the weeks around an election. Two 24-hour suspensions in August and September 2001 are not shown.

Dates are taken from Wikipedia's article on the Northern Ireland Executive.
